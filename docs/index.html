Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Ferry is open source under the MIT license. No trial, no Pro version, no upsell.

Why does it need Accessibility?

To list your windows, notice the shortcut and bring the window you pick forward. Without it a window switcher can’t switch windows.

Is it recording my screen?

No. Screen Recording is optional and only used to take one small still picture of a window when the switcher or a Dock preview is about to show it. Pictures stay in memory, are dropped when the window closes, and are never saved or sent anywhere. Skip the permission and Ferry shows app icons instead.

Can it replace ⌘Tab?

Yes. Pick ⌘ in Settings and Ferry takes over ⌘Tab while it runs, then hands it back to macOS when you quit.

Can I use it next to AltTab or DockDoor?

Better not at the same time: they listen for the same shortcut and the same Dock hover. Pick one.

macOS says it can’t be opened.

Builds aren’t notarized yet. Right-click Ferry and choose Open, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Ferry.app.

Does the little ferry on the wallpaper do anything?

It moves along a bit every time you switch windows. That’s it. That’s the feature.
